Interpretation: This is a qualitative, reduced-order teaching model—not a finite-element solver. It illustrates gravity load paths, story drift, approximate P‑Δ softening, member capacity loss, and impact-driven progression. It omits real geometry, code-calibrated material properties, connections, soil, and many failure modes. Do not use it to assess a real building.
The colors show a simplified utilization estimate. Red indicates the model has reached or exceeded its assumed member capacity; it does not predict a real building's safety.
