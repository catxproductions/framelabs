Interpretation: This is a qualitative, reduced-order teaching model. Material capacities, mass, load paths, impact energy and integrity are illustrative units, not calibrated real-world properties or engineering calculations. The model can demonstrate weak points and cascading failure, but it does not validate actual building safety.
Tips: Build a few continuous columns from ground level, connect them with beams, add slabs, then select Validate. Apply loads to individual bays and observe the heatmap. Use Demolish to remove members, then Simulate to observe gravity and impact transfer. Save JSON exports the current design, including component material and integrity.
